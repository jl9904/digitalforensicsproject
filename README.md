# Digital Forensics Project
The project option for Digital Forensics. Examined evidence and showcased digital forensic skills. Using the Chapter 04 - Charlie work USB file that was available through Cengage.

🔍 Evidence

Evidence includes Charlie, the person who's USB I was allowed access to, using his work email to contact other people outside the company to offer them intel on his current company. He's also using the company email to discuss his dislike for his boss. Even using the email to talk about simple things about getting coffee with coworkers. Moreover, he even falls for some scams here and there and forwards the possible scam to his coworkers 

Where I got this information: Email artifacts recovered during disk analysis, using the Chapter 04 - Charlie work USB file that was available through Cengage.

🛠️ Tools Used
Autopsy

Using Autopsy, I selected it because of the ease of analyzing the drive through Autopsy. It allows multiple ingest modules to run, getting multiple things done at once and not having to repeat any steps along the way. Additionally, the ease of access when it comes to seeing what goes on in the emails or the images, you don't need an external source to identify anything specific. Lastly, it's free and open source, so I saved expenses, it's simple to use once you know the basic steps to follow to view information.

According to the web:
Able to parse email artifacts, images, browser history, and more
Supportive of automated ingest modules for streamlined workflows

✔️ Results

Challenges I encountered were having quite a few emails to go through and even some images. A few encrypted files that the analyzers went through and broke down. Lastly, I would say the main challenge was connecting the pieces of evidence together to truly prove that Charlie was misusing his work email and furthermore, though just speculation, work computer. 

Most elements of the analysis functioned as expected.

👍 Recommendation

I absolutely recommend Autopsy as a tool as it is a free tool to analyze disks/vms that contain information about cases. It was a solid approach that gave enough detail to figure out what was going on in the case. It allowed me to run multiple ingests at once and compiled the information in a way that was organized well and simple to digest.

Autopsy proved reliable, efficient, and well-suited for this investigation.
