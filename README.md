# Digital Forensics Project
The project option for Digital Forensics. Examined evidence and showcased digital forensic skills. Using the Chapter 04 - Charlie work USB file that was available through Cengage.

🔍 Evidence Summary

Evidence includes Charlie using his work email to contact other people outside the company to offer them intel on his current company. He's also using the company email to discuss his dislike for his boss. Even using the email to talk about simple things about getting coffee with coworkers.

Source: Email artifacts recovered during disk analysis.

🛠️ Tools Used
Autopsy

Using Autopsy, I selected it because of the ease of analyzing the drive through Autopsy. It allows multiple ingest modules to run, getting multiple things done at once and not having to repeat any steps along the way.

Free and open-source

Beginner-friendly and widely used in digital forensics

Able to parse email artifacts, images, browser history, and more

Supportive of automated ingest modules for streamlined workflows

✔️ Results – Did Things Work as Expected?

Challenges I encountered were having quite a few emails to go through and even some images. A few encrypted files that the analyzers went through and broke down.

Most elements of the analysis functioned as expected.

👍 Recommendation

I absolutely recommend Autopsy as a tool as it is a free tool to analyze disks/vms that contain information about cases. It was a solid approach that gave enough detail to figure out what was going on in the case.

Autopsy proved reliable, efficient, and well-suited for this investigation.
